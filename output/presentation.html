01 · Problem Statement

The Hardware Prototyping Trap

Why 65%+ student and maker robotics projects fail before first assembly:

Danger
High-Voltage Disasters
Beginners inadvertently mix 220V mains or incompatible battery discharge rates with 3.3V/5V logic pins, frying microcontrollers or causing fire hazards.
Hallucination
Phantom Component Pricing
Standard LLMs (ChatGPT/Claude) guess costs from outdated foreign blogs. Teams budget ₹1,000 only to find out parts cost ₹3,500 locally or are out of stock.
Dead Ends
Pin & Power Mismatches
Conflicting I2C addresses, unlevel-shifted 5V sensors burning 3.3V ESP32 GPIOs, and missing pull-up resistors stall teams for days.
