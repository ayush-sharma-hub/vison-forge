## Safety

- Mains AC voltage (110V-230V): Use an isolated industrial SMPS, GFCI breaker, and fused relay optocouplers with zero exposed conductors.
- 220V AC electricity: Requires optoisolated relays, PCB creepage distance >6mm, and certified earthed metal enclosure.
- High-voltage subsystem: Must incorporate galvanic isolation, flyback diodes, and dedicated earthing.
- Laser diodes: Use certified safety goggles (OD 5+ rating) matched to the laser wavelength and active beam warning indicators.
